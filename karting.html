Overview

Main deliverable of the Expressió Gràfica 2 course at EPSEM (UPC Manresa), completed during my Erasmus exchange. The goal was to produce a complete CAD assembly of a kart — from individual part modeling through to full assembly and production drawings.

Standard components (seat, steering wheel, engine, wheels) were sourced from manufacturer databases and integrated into the assembly, consistent with how structural design actually works — supplier geometry anchors your layout, you design everything around it.

Parts designed from scratch

  • Tubular steel chassis — space frame geometry, sized for rigidity and kart-spec compliance
  • Rear disc brake system — disc, caliper mounts, master cylinder bracket
  • Steering hub and front knuckles — stub axles, kingpin geometry, tie rod attachment
  • Rear axle assembly — solid axle with bearing carriers and sprocket interface
  • Aerodynamic bodywork — surfacic front fairing and rear panels, mounting directly onto the chassis frame

Aerodynamic surfaces

Designed using the Generative Shape Design workbench in CATIA V5 — surfacic modeling rather than solid. The forms aren't CFD-optimized at this stage, but they're built to mount correctly: proper datum references, realistic clearances, and coherent interface geometry with the frame tubes.

A deliberate step beyond the course scope, and a first applied exercise in the direction of aerodynamic design on constrained vehicle geometry.

Engineering drawings

The drawing set covers individual parts with tolerances and material callouts, sub-assemblies with exploded views and parts lists, and full assembly views with section cuts — all following ISO projection standards and UPC title block conventions.

Skills applied

  • CATIA V5 — Part Design, Assembly Design, Generative Shape Design
  • Engineering drawing to ISO standards — projections, tolerancing, section views
  • Assembly architecture — kinematic constraints, DOF analysis, interference checking
  • Integration of supplier STEP geometry into original assemblies
  • Surfacic aerodynamic modeling on constrained chassis geometry
