About

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student from UTBM (France), currently building on an Erasmus exchange at UPC EPSEM in Manresa, Spain.

My goal is to work in motorsport aerodynamics — the physics of flow fields, boundary layers, and downforce generation, and the CAD/CFD tools used to act on them.

Outside the lab, I've spent 14 years racing mountain bikes competitively, which is where a lot of my instinct for vehicle dynamics and endurance under pressure comes from.
